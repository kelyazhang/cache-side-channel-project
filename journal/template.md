# Week N Learning Journal

Use one entry per meaningful work session. Keep this file chronological and
link formal measurements to `experiments/weekN/`.

## YYYY-MM-DD - Entry Title

### Context

What was being tested, implemented, or read?

### Observation or Problem

What happened? Include measurements, errors, or unexpected behavior.

### Investigation

Which explanations were considered, and which checks were performed?

### Resolution or Current Understanding

What was fixed, confirmed, or left unresolved?

### Reflection and Next Action

What was learned, and what is the next concrete step?
